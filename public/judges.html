PaddyPay helps rice cooperatives track alternate wetting and drying (AWD) while the crop is growing, preserve evidence that can be checked independently, and share simulated carbon revenue by a published rule.

What the prototype proves

  • Every simulated sensor reading carries an EIP-191 device signature.
  • Daily Merkle roots were anchored in a local smart contract. The public snapshot preserves the roots, signatures and proofs; one altered reading fails cryptographic verification.
  • A plot can be registered only once per season; a duplicate claim was mined and reverted.
  • Only green plots were finalized. Simulated dVND is allocated 70% farmer, 10% cooperative, 20% project developer.

Scope and assumptions. This is a public snapshot of a hackathon prototype designed around VM0051 v1.1 requirements. The local chain is not reachable from this deployment. All readings, images, token payments, and the VND 150,000/tCO₂e sale price in the An Giang season are simulated or assumed. A separate case study shows published 2023 field measurements from Spain; those measurements were not collected by PaddyPay and do not support payouts. The simplified QA3 estimate deducts 15% uncertainty and omits N₂O adjustment, sampling uncertainty, and independent verification. No carbon credits are issued by PaddyPay.
